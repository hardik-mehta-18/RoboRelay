Webhook delivery

Optional. Sent as an Authorization: Bearer header and stored in the agent's local settings. Readings from a topic that arrive sooner than this are dropped, keeping only the latest. 1000 keeps about one per second; 0 keeps every message. Batches of up to 100 are sent as {"events": [...]}. Use 1 for a generic webhook that expects one plain event per request.

MQTT delivery

MQTT is optional. Pending readings go to the currently configured destinations.

Discovered ROS 2 nodes and services

Running nodes and the services they offer are included in a periodic snapshot sent to your destinations. Parameter services are left out.
